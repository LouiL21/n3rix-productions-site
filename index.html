Script to screen.

N3RIX Productions is a personal, self-hosted video studio. It turns written scripts into short animated vertical videos and, after the owner reviews and approves each one, uploads it to the owner's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X and LinkedIn.

How it uses your accounts

  • It only uploads videos and captions that you created and approved yourself.
  • It never posts automatically without your approval in the review queue.
  • It does not read, collect or share your contacts, messages, viewing history or any other account data.
  • It runs on your own computer. There is no N3RIX Productions server or cloud service.
N3RIX Productions is a personal tool and is not affiliated with Google, YouTube, TikTok, Meta, X or LinkedIn. Contact: github.com/LouiL21.
