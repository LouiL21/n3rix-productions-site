Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Summary

N3RIX Productions is a personal application that runs entirely on its owner's own computer. It does not collect, sell or share personal data, and it has no servers of its own.

What the app accesses

When you connect a social account (for example YouTube through Google sign-in), the app asks only for permission to upload videos to your account and to read basic channel information needed to confirm the upload. For YouTube these are the youtube.upload and youtube.readonly scopes.

How data is used

  • Videos, titles and captions you approve are sent only to the platform you choose to publish to.
  • The app does not read or store your contacts, messages, viewing history, subscribers' data or analytics.
  • No data is used for advertising, profiling or training AI models.

How data is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored only on your own computer, encrypted for your user account, and are never sent anywhere except back to the platform that issued them. Nothing is stored by N3RIX Productions anywhere else.

Google user data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can disconnect any account in the app at any time, which deletes its stored token. You can also revoke access directly: for Google, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions. Other platforms offer the same in their account or app settings.
